Lake Fishing

Escape into the serene beauty of the Alpine lakes, surrounded by snow-capped peaks and lush green forests. Our lake fishing trips offer the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation, with expert guides helping you reel in trout, perch, and char from crystal-clear mountain waters. Whether fishing from the shore or drifting on a boat, this is a peaceful yet rewarding experience for nature lovers and anglers alike.

Cost

A half-day fishing trip, including equipment and permits, starts at £80 per person, while a full-day guided experience with lunch is available from £150 per person.

Itinerary

  • 6:30 AM – Meet your guide and receive fishing gear.
  • 7:00 AM – Cast your line into the pristine alpine waters.
  • 10:00 AM – Mid-morning coffee break with local pastries.
  • 12:00 PM – End of half-day trip (for those on half-day option).
  • 1:00 PM – Shoreline lunch featuring fresh local ingredients.
  • 2:30 PM – Boat fishing and advanced casting techniques.
  • 5:00 PM – Wrap-up, optional fish cleaning service, and relaxation by the lake.
