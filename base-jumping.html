Base Jumping

For the ultimate thrill-seekers, our base jumping experience offers the chance to leap from the stunning cliffs and peaks of the French and Swiss Alps. With the wind rushing past and panoramic mountain views below, this is the pinnacle of extreme sports. Our expert instructors provide comprehensive training and safety support, making it possible for first-timers to take the plunge in one of the most breathtaking locations in the world.

Cost

This high-adrenaline adventure, including training, equipment, and instructor-led jumps, starts at £350 per person.

Itinerary

  • 7:00 AM – Registration, safety briefing, and training on parachute control.
  • 9:00 AM – Practice jumps from a training platform.
  • 12:30 PM – Lunch with incredible mountain views.
  • 2:00 PM – Take your first real base jump, guided by expert instructors.
  • 4:00 PM – Video review, debrief, and celebration at a local alpine lodge.
