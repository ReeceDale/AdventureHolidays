Road Cycling

Theres no better way to explore the French and Swiss Alps than on two wheels. Ride through picturesque mountain villages, challenge yourself on legendary Tour de France climbs, and cruise down breathtaking alpine descents. Whether you’re a casual cyclist or a seasoned pro, our guided road cycling tours are designed to suit all fitness levels, ensuring an unforgettable journey through some of the most stunning landscapes in Europe.

Cost

A full-day guided cycling tour, including a support vehicle, refreshments, and expert route planning, starts at £90 per person. Bike rentals are available for an additional £30 per day, or you can bring your own.

Itinerary

  • 7:00 AM – Meet your guide for a route briefing and bike check.
  • 8:00 AM – Ride through alpine valleys with gentle warm-up climbs.
  • 10:00 AM – Stop at a traditional mountain café for a break.
  • 11:30 AM – Take on a famous Tour de France climb or enjoy a rolling scenic route.
  • 1:00 PM – Lunch at a local chalet with stunning mountain views.
  • 2:30 PM – Enjoy a thrilling descent or a relaxed afternoon ride.
  • 4:00 PM – Return to base, cool down, and celebrate with a post-ride drink.
