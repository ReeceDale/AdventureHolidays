Rock Climbing

Take your adventure to new heights with an unforgettable rock climbing experience in the heart of the French and Swiss Alps. Whether you're scaling towering limestone cliffs in Chamonix or conquering the rugged granite walls of the Swiss Jura, our guided climbing trips offer a perfect blend of adrenaline and breathtaking alpine scenery. With routes suitable for all levels, from beginner-friendly crags to expert multi-pitch ascents, this is the perfect challenge for climbers looking to push their limits.

Cost

The cost for a full-day guided climbing experience, including safety gear and expert instruction, starts at £120 per person.

Itinerary

  • 8:00 AM – Meet your guide, gear up, and receive a safety briefing.
  • 9:00 AM – Begin climbing on routes tailored to your skill level.
  • 12:00 PM – Enjoy a scenic picnic lunch overlooking the alpine valleys.
  • 1:30 PM – Continue climbing with a mix of bouldering, top-roping, and lead climbing.
  • 4:00 PM – Descend back to base, debrief, and relax with a well-earned après-climb refreshment.
